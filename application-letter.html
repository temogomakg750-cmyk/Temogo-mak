Application Letter

A formal letter applying for an industrial attachment.

Temogo Makgalemela
Francistown
Botswana

15 July 2026

The Company Manager
Debswana Diamond Company
Gaborone
Botswana

Subject: APPLICATION FOR AN INDUSTRIAL ATTACHMENT

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to express my interest in an industrial attachment opportunity at your company. I am a student at Botswana Accountancy College in Francistown, studying Computer Systems Engineering. Every student must complete an attachment in order to graduate.

For that reason, I humbly request an attachment slot in your highly esteemed company, in the computer systems laboratory, to enhance my practical computer engineering skills, starting on 3 August 2027. I have gained theoretical knowledge about computer systems, and this opportunity will help me to apply what I have learned in class, to acquire highly valuable skills, and to contribute towards the achievement of the company's goals and vision.

Your company has the resources that will help me put my theoretical knowledge into practice and develop expert skills. I believe I will be an asset to your company, as I am a highly motivated and hardworking student.

My CV and the letter from my institution are attached for your review. I look forward to being considered for the attachment programme. Thank you.

Yours faithfully,


Temogo Makgalemela
