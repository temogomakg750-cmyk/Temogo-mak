Reflective Blog: Group Work Experience

How my group progressed through Tuckman's stages of group development this semester.

1Forming

In the forming stage we introduced ourselves to each other so that we could get to know one another and get along well. This went very successfully. We talked about the goals we wanted to achieve and discussed how the group would operate.

We faced some difficulties, such as feeling comfortable around each other, because it was our first time meeting and some of us had never worked in a serious group like this one. The culture was new to us. Comfort matters in a group because if members are not comfortable it is difficult for them to contribute, but when everyone is comfortable, contributing becomes easy.

Responsibilities were allocated to every member. As a group we also set group rules and agreed on the goals we wanted to achieve.

2Storming

During storming we faced problems. Some members were unwilling to take on any responsibility, some were unhappy with the positions they had been given and wanted to change them, and some were competing for authority in the group.

In the end we found solutions. We learned how to resolve conflict calmly and respectfully, and we were able to allocate responsibilities again.

3Norming

In this stage we began by learning how to solve the conflicts and problems we were facing, such as members not attending group meetings, competition for leadership positions, and some members not feeling comfortable around others. We learned to handle these matters calmly and respectfully, which brought peace and cooperation to the group.

We also agreed on the platforms our group would use to discuss group matters: WhatsApp and Microsoft Teams. We did not want anyone to be confused about where to communicate, or to end up using many platforms, because that makes group communication difficult to manage.

4Performing

The group performed very well. Group rules were followed and every member carried out their duties and roles. Everyone was cooperative, and the group leaders played a big part in managing discussions and the group itself, which brought our performance to its peak.

When we faced problems, we made sure they were solved quickly, because spending too long on problems wastes time and lowers performance. We also kept looking for ways to help us reach the goals we had set.

5Adjourning

Group assignments were completed on time by every member, and preparations for presentations were done while there was still time. When results were released we often performed best, and when we performed poorly we felt hurt and emotional.

We celebrated our achievements. Sometimes we hosted a small party to relax after the exhaustion of group assignments, sharing dinner and talking about our group's performance.

What I Learned

From every project and assignment we learned new skills, and we learned how to improve our group for future projects. Most importantly, I learned that clear communication, agreed rules, shared responsibility and respectful conflict resolution are what make a group effective, and that being ready for the next group project starts with learning from the last one.
