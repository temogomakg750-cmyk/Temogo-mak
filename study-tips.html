Study Tips

Strategies that have worked for me as a first-year student.

Break Tasks Down

Split large assignments into smaller, manageable tasks so that the work feels achievable and nothing is left until the last minute.

Use a Weekly Timetable

Plan lectures, personal study time and assignment work for the whole week, and follow the plan consistently.

Set Early Deadlines

Choose a personal completion date before the official deadline. This leaves time to check your work and submit calmly.

Prepare Before Lectures

Read ahead and plan each evening. Consistent daily preparation is more effective than studying only when I feel like it.

Study With Others

Build good relationships with course mates. Friendly classmates make it easier to ask for help and to learn together.

Put in Real Effort

Good results come from consistent effort. Treat each module as something to work at, not something that will take care of itself.
