Reflective Blog: My Learning Journey

Time management, adapting to tertiary education, study habits, and growth mindset.

Time Management

I had difficulty managing my time, and this affected me negatively. I was not able to write some of the proposed quizzes, and I did not get enough time to prepare for my exams, attend to lectures and study. This led to poor performance.

I decided to find a solution by researching ways in which a student can manage time. I learned that I should break larger tasks into smaller, manageable ones. I also learned to create a weekly timetable that includes lectures and my own study time, and to set an assignment completion time ahead of each deadline. This helped me to submit and complete assignments on time. The approach worked, because my performance started to improve once I put my research findings into use.

Adapting to Tertiary School

Adapting was not easy, as pressure and workload increased. I found it difficult to adjust to my course because I had never studied computer studies before. I also had new course mates, and getting to know them and socialising with them was not easy. This mattered, because it makes it easier to ask for help when I need it and it promotes a peaceful environment.

Adapting to the campus itself was easy, as I had visited it before. In the end I was able to cope with my course and to build friendships with my course mates, and I now feel comfortable around them.

Study Habits

My study habits had to change. I was used to a system in which I studied only when I felt like it, but at college the work piles up every day. I developed a new routine in which I read, study, plan and prepare for lectures consistently, including at night.

In senior school we had no assignments, so I also had to adapt to assignments being introduced. I am now used to these changes and they no longer affect me the way they did at first.

Growth and Mindset

I had to change my belief that someone can perform well without putting in much effort, because that approach had worked for me before. I now understand that to pass I need to put in real effort and study very hard.

Along the way I have learned new skills, including programming, communication, studying skills and socialising with other people, which contributes to a peaceful learning environment.
